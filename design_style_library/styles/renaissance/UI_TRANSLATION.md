# Renaissance — UI translation

## Structure
Translate `linear perspective and architectural depth` and `measured bilateral balance` into layout, information hierarchy and component geometry before color or ornament.

## Components
Navigation, charts, filters, tables and state indicators must remain legible; the style changes their relationships, not only their skin.

## Intensity
- **LOW**: one or two core compositional cues in a mature product shell.
- **MEDIUM**: composition, typography rhythm and color roles visibly follow the DNA.
- **HIGH**: strong spatial grammar while retaining navigation, KPIs, chart, list, table and states.

## Curation rule
Pairwise distinction is a selection criterion, not permission to add noise. Keep the most elegant, historically coherent and usable candidate; discard weaker near-duplicates.
