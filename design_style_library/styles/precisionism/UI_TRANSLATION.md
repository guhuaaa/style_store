# Precisionism — UI translation

## Structure
Translate `clean industrial silhouette` and `crisp planar reduction` into information hierarchy, grouping, and component geometry before ornament.

## Intensity
- **LOW**: restrained mature product shell with one or two compositional cues.
- **MEDIUM**: composition, typography rhythm, and color roles visibly follow the DNA.
- **HIGH**: strong spatial grammar while retaining navigation, KPIs, charts, lists, tables, and states.

## Curation rule
Keep only visually distinct, elegant, and usable candidates. Discard near-duplicates rather than adding noise.
